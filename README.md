# 📊 HR Analytics Power BI Dashboard

A comprehensive Data Analytics project built using **Power BI Project (`.pbip`)** to analyze workforce dynamics, track key human resource metrics, and empower HR leadership with data-driven decision-making.

---

## 📌 Business Overview

Human Resource departments face challenges in managing workforce attrition, optimizing employee performance, and ensuring equitable compensation. This interactive dashboard addresses these challenges by transforming raw HR data into actionable organizational insights.

### Key Objectives
* **Attrition Analysis:** Identify voluntary and involuntary turnover drivers.
* **Demographics & Diversity:** Evaluate workforce composition across age, gender, and departments.
* **Employee Satisfaction:** Correlate job roles, environment satisfaction, and work-life balance with retention.
* **Compensation & Promotion:** Measure salary distributions and growth opportunities across job tiers.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **Total Headcount:** Total active workforce.
- **Attrition Rate (%):** Percentage of employees leaving the organization.
- **Average Tenure:** Mean duration of employee service.
- **Average Monthly Income:** Overall and role-based compensation benchmarking.
- **Job Satisfaction Score:** Ratings on workplace environment and role fulfillment.
